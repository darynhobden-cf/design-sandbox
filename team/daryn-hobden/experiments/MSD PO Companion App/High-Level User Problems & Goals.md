Here are the high-level user problems and goals for a companion app designed to help pet owners manage their pet's health, specifically focusing on chronic issues like atopic dermatitis (itch):

  

High-Level User Problems (Pain Points)

- Information Overload and Confusion: Pet owners face an abundance of conflicting online information, leaving them overwhelmed and unsure of who to trust. They struggle to determine if their dog's behavior is normal or indicative of a serious problem, and find it difficult to digest complex diagnostic information from short vet visits.
    
- Emotional Distress and Fatigue: The journey is highly emotional; owners feel guilt for potentially overlooking early signs or causing compliance slips, anxiety about the seriousness of the issue, and profound emotional fatigue from being stuck in a trial-and-error loop of treatments.
    
- Treatment Frustration and Compliance Friction: Owners worry about administering treatments correctly and fear potential side effects or behavioral changes. The unpredictability of flare-ups and the inconvenience of maintaining a strict, consistent care routine long-term causes significant frustration.
    
- Financial Strain: The ongoing costs of emergency vet visits, testing multiple treatments, and budgeting for the lifelong management of a chronic condition create a heavy financial burden.
    

High-Level User Goals (Needs)

- Obtain Reliable, Actionable Guidance: Users want access to simple diagnostic tools to assess severity, jargon-free explanations of potential causes and side effects, and step-by-step guidance on how to administer treatments and handle home remedies.
    
- Track and Evaluate Health Progress: Owners need tools, such as digital diaries, to track symptoms and evaluate the effectiveness of different treatments over time so they can clearly see what works and what doesn't.
    
- Manage Care Routines and Prevent Flare-Ups: Users aim to establish a consistent long-term care routine using management tools like digital reminders to prevent compliance slips. When flare-ups do occur, they need rapid-response solutions and access to professional advice for fast relief.
    
- Maximize Veterinary Consultations: Owners want to feel prepared for short vet visits. They need non-judgmental guidance on exactly what to ask and share with their vet to ensure they get an effective diagnosis and treatment plan.
    
- Access Financial Support and Community: Users seek loyalty schemes and financial rebates to help make long-term care affordable. They also want to connect with a community of peers facing similar chronic conditions to find moral support, read authentic reviews, and share their own experiences.
