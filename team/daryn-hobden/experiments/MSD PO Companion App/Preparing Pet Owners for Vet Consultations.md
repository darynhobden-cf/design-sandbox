To help pet owners prepare for vet visits, the app can provide practical tools and educational content designed to reduce anxiety and make short appointments more effective. Here is how it can help:

- Symptom Checklists and Triage: The app can offer pre-diagnosis "itch checklists" and simple diagnostic tools to help owners evaluate the severity of the condition and confidently decide when it is time to book a vet appointment. A dedicated pre-vet visit checklist can also ensure they are ready for the consultation.
    
- Discussion Guides: To maximize the value of the consultation, the app can provide non-judgemental guides and articles detailing exactly what to ask the vet and what specific information to share about their pet's history. This helps owners prepare for the exact questions the vet is likely to ask them.
    
- Setting Clear Expectations: The app can feature content explaining what will happen at the clinic, how the step-by-step diagnosis process works, and what initial treatment options might look like. This prepares owners for the reality that diagnosing an itch often involves a "trial and error" phase and that the condition may require lifelong management.
    
- Reassurance and Emotional Support: By providing empathetic reassurance that skin conditions affect many dogs and are not a result of poor owner care, the app can alleviate the guilt and panic pet owners often feel before walking into the clinic.
