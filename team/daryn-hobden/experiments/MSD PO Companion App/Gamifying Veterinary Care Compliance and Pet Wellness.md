Gamified tracking tools can be implemented to offer rewards when users successfully complete specific treatment milestones. An app can feature a points system that allows users to earn redeemable points for vet care or exclusive merchandise, with the ability to unlock higher earning potential as they climb new tiers.

  

Apps can also use interactive, daily "To-do" lists that integrate routine care tasks—such as walking the dog, feeding, and refilling the water bowl—alongside medication administration. Pairing these daily checklists with a condition tracker helps visualize the dog's progress over time, which reinforces the value of sticking to the routine.

  

Gamification can also extend to community involvement through an ambassador program. Users can earn rewards or perks by sharing "before and after" photos, writing testimonials, and referring friends.

  

Finally, these gamified tracking elements and rewards can be combined with bite-sized educational stories to keep owners engaged and continuously learning about their pet's health.
