Business Problems

- Competitor Market Dominance: The dermatology market has been heavily dominated by a major competitor (Zoetis, with products like Apoquel and Cytopoint) for 10 years. These competitors already own the space and have established reward programs and vet-led education, making market penetration highly challenging.
    
- Lack of Direct Pet Owner Engagement: Existing strategies and customer journey maps have focused almost exclusively on veterinarians, creating a massive gap in understanding pet owner needs and reaching them directly during the pre-diagnosis phase.
    
- Siloed Operations and Poor Data Integration: Current internal systems are not omnichannel; they rely heavily on manual processes and sales representatives. There is limited data on qualified pet owners, a lack of lead generation systems, and missing workflows between marketing and sales teams.
    
- Treatment Compliance Slips: Pet owners often have a low awareness of the chronic nature of the condition and face significant financial strain. As a result, they frequently stop treatment outside of peak allergy seasons or when symptoms briefly improve, leading to compliance slips that negatively impact both the pet's health and sustained business revenue.
    
- Regulatory Roadblocks: Navigating regulatory challenges is a significant blocker, particularly the need to create tailored marketing strategies that balance branded campaigns (e.g., in the US) with unbranded, educational approaches (e.g., in Europe) to remain compliant.
    

Business Outcomes

- Market Penetration and Competitor Replacement: The primary goal for Year 1 is to launch the product successfully with veterinarians, drive penetration and occupancy across key accounts, build trust, and actively replace competitor products.
    
- Increased Treatment Compliance and Retention: A critical outcome is to improve long-term treatment adherence rates and rebate program participation. This will be achieved by deploying behavioral compliance nudges (like digital reminders) and offering simple, user-friendly digital loyalty programs.
    
- Clear Market Differentiation: Successfully distinguishing the brand by emphasizing its key differentiators: its second-generation status, a superior safety profile that allows it to be given to puppies 6 to 12 months old, and simplified pricing models that remove complexity.
    
- Seamless Omnichannel Integration and Lead Conversion: Connecting digital and offline touchpoints to create a cohesive ecosystem. This involves feeding marketing and clickstream data directly into sales workflows to improve follow-up efficiency, track customer behavior, and ultimately increase lead conversion rates.
    
- Establishment as a Trusted Partner: Building a brand reputation as stable and supportive. By using empathy-driven storytelling and accessible educational tools, the business aims to make veterinarians feel supported and pet owners feel confident, informed, and hopeful.
