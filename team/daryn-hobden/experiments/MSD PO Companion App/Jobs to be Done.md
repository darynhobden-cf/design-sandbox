Here are the main Jobs to be Done (JTBD) for a pet owner managing their pet's health, categorized by the different phases of their journey:

  

Assess and Triage Symptoms

- Determine if a pet's behavior is normal or problematic, and understand the different types of itch, such as temporary, seasonal, or chronic.
    
- Assess the severity of symptoms using simple diagnostic tools to know exactly when to escalate the issue and book a vet appointment.
    
- Find reliable, easy-to-understand guidance on home remedies, holistic options, and over-the-counter products to provide initial relief before seeing a professional.
    

Navigate Vet Consultations and Diagnoses

- Prepare for vet visits by accessing non-judgmental guidance on what specific topics and symptoms to discuss with the practice.
    
- Understand the diagnosis process and treatment options through clear, jargon-free explanations of potential causes, treatment benefits, side effects, and long-term costs.
    

Administer and Track Treatments

- Learn how to properly administer treatments through step-by-step explanations and helpful tips.
    
- Track and evaluate the effectiveness of different treatments over time to help establish what works best during the "trial and error" phase.
    
- Maintain strict treatment compliance using care routine management tools, such as digital reminders, to stay consistent and avoid medication slips.
    

Manage Flare-Ups and Communicate with Vets

- Monitor symptoms and learn when to follow up with the vet, making it easier to accurately communicate the evolution of the condition and the impact of treatments.
    
- Access rapid-response solutions and professional advice when flare-ups occur to minimize the dog's distress, offer fast relief, and identify potential environmental or dietary triggers.
    

Manage the Financial Impact of Long-Term Care

- Budget for long-term care by easily accessing financial rebates and loyalty schemes that help make ongoing management affordable and encourage good compliance.
    

Connect with a Community and Stay Informed

- Find moral support and read personal reviews by connecting with a support system or community of other pet owners who are facing the same chronic issues.
    
- Share personal stories and guidance with others once they have adapted to their pet's long-term new normal.
    
- Easily access research updates and news about evolving treatments that might be more effective or affordable in the future.
