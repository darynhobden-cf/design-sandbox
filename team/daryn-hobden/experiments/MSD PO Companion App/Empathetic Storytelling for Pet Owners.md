To build trust with pet owners, emotional storytelling should be empathy-driven, highlighting the profound emotional impact of chronic conditions on both pets and their families. Because owners view their dogs as family members and often feel a heavy burden of responsibility, narratives should focus on restoring the human-animal bond and showing how treatments bring relief, returning pets to their normal comfort and daily activities.

  

Key approaches include:

- Sharing Relatable Peer Experiences: 
  
  Using video case studies, user-generated content, and testimonials from other pet owners who have navigated the same difficult journey is highly effective. Featuring authentic stories about discovering an itch or managing unpredictable flare-ups provides vital moral support and reassures owners that they are not alone in their struggles.
    
- Adopting a Supportive, Non-Judgmental Tone: 
  
  Storytelling must acknowledge the guilt, anxiety, and emotional fatigue owners frequently experience. It is crucial that the content is soothing and completely avoids any language, tone, or messaging that might induce fear, guilt, or shame regarding their pet's condition or their caretaking abilities.
    
- Blending Emotion with Education: 
  
  Emotional stories can be woven together with clear, educational content—such as vet and Key Opinion Leader (KOL) explainers—to create a supportive ecosystem. This balance ensures the storytelling is not only deeply relatable but also informative, trustworthy, and actionable, empowering pet owners to take the best next steps.
